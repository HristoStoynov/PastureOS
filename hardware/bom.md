# Bill of Materials (example options to evaluate)

Do not treat these as fixed part numbers — they are example categories to evaluate.

- GNSS module (u-blox or Quectel evaluation modules)
- MCU: ultra-low-power ARM Cortex-M (e.g., STM32L4 series) or Nordic nRF52 for BLE+MCU
- Modem: LTE-M / NB-IoT module or LoRaWAN transceiver
- IMU: 3-axis accelerometer + gyroscope (e.g., ICM-20649)
- Temp sensor: digital temperature sensor or thermistor
- Speaker / buzzer: waterproofed
- Battery: Li-ion or LiFePO4 with protection circuit
- Solar charge controller (optional)
