# Welfare and safety protocol

Principles
- Audio-first learning: animals are trained to respond to audio cues before pulses are enabled.
- Pulse as last resort and minimised: pulses must be rare, short, low-intensity and configurable.
- Farmer override and stop: farmers can immediately disable cues/pulses per device or herd.

Training requirements
- Training period before full deployment (see pilot plan)
- Record training completion per animal

Event & escalation
- Log every cue and pulse with timestamp, location and battery/firmware state
- If high distress indicators (rapid acceleration, prolonged vocalisation, collapse) occur, escalate to human review and vet

Ethics
- AI is non-diagnostic; it flags candidate anomalies for vet/farmer review only.
